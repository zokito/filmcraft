  (`EngineError::Disabled`, crates/engine/src/lib.rs ~79, ~616-626, ~672-695; CLI
  apps/filmcraft-cli/src/main.rs ~226-232). Keep that.
- Preconditions are named predicates `fn(&Session) -> Result<(), String>`
  (crates/engine/src/commands.rs ~20, ~32, ~75-110: has_seq, has_selection,
  has_project_selection, has_source, has_in_out). Messages say what is missing, not what to run.
- `command.list` (commands.rs ~2470) emits `"enabled": (c.enabled)(s).is_ok()` and drops the
  reason; `describe` prints that row.
- `inspect_sequence` (commands.rs ~3109-3134) emits tracks, transitions, markers, not captions
  (`caption_tracks`, crates/project/src/lib.rs ~929, caption.rs ~167).
